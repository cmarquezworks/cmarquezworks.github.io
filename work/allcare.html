AllCare bundles worker benefits (HMO, life insurance, teleconsultation, dental care and more) into one membership. I led the redesign of the public site and the whole logged-in side, then built both inside the Rails app.

I last worked on this in 2024; the live site may have changed since. The screenshot shows my version.

  1. secondary action, outlined
  2. dashboard: one action per card
  3. headline → detail → one button

The brief

Make a benefits platform easy to understand and easy to use: public pages that tell people what they get, and a logged-in area where members find each benefit and act on it.

What I did

  1. Redesigned the public landing pages around one headline, a plain list of what is covered, and one primary button.
  2. Designed the logged-in side, the MyBenefits dashboard: each benefit is a card with a single action, such as requesting a letter of guarantee.
  3. Built the screens inside the Ruby on Rails app, responsive from phone to desktop.
  4. Tightened usability and layout across the platform, from the landing pages to the account screens.

What shipped
