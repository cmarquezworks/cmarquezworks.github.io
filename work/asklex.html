Ask Lex PH Academy runs courses, mentoring and events for students and professionals. I led the UI/UX design of the platform and supported its frontend build in Ruby on Rails.

I last worked on this in 2024; the live site may have changed since. The screenshot shows my version.

  1. the only filled button
  2. proof row: figure, label, context
  3. browse by category or by mentor

The brief

Give a growing catalogue of courses a clear shape, so a visitor can browse by topic or by mentor and register without hunting for the button.

What I did

  1. Led the interface design of the platform, with clarity and ease of use as the test for every screen.
  2. Set the catalogue up to be browsed two ways, by category or by mentor, as two tabs.
  3. Kept one filled button in the header, Register, so the main action is never in doubt.
  4. Supported the frontend build inside the Rails app.

What shipped
