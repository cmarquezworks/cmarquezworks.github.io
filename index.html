03 / Process

How a page gets made.

  1. 01

    Understand

    What does the business need this page to do? I start there, with who is reading and on what screen.

  2. 02

    Design

    Layout, type and colour in Figma, tried against the real content, never placeholder text.

  3. 03

    Spec

    Every size, gap and colour written down, the way this page shows its own. Nothing left to guess.

  4. 04

    Build

    Responsive HTML and CSS in the real stack: Rails views, WordPress or Webflow. Then I check it on real phones.

04 / About

Nine years on both sides of the handoff.

I started in 2014 making banners and fixing layouts, and I have worked on the web ever since. Across five teams I've been the designer, the frontend developer, and often both on the same project.

At GSS Labs I led frontend for more than four years, building WordPress sites and keeping them stable. At Digiteer I led UI/UX and frontend from design to deployment and guided junior designers, then moved to frontend work across many client sites.

After Digiteer I spent a year on TikTok as a content creator, making short videos with AI tools and reading the numbers on every one. It taught me how fast people decide to stay or scroll, which is the same question every landing page has to answer.
